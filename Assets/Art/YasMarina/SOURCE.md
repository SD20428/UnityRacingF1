The source file is retained verbatim as source.geojson. Its filename refers to the circuit's opening year; the downloaded geometry and metadata describe the 5,281m layout with the reprofiled northern hairpin and southern curved section.

Reference: FIA Abu Dhabi Grand Prix preview, https://www.fia.com/sites/default/files/2022_abu_dhabi_gp_preview_0.pdf — post-2021 layout, 5.281km. The FIA material is used for factual verification only and is not bundled as an asset.

Modifications: longitude/latitude converted to a local equirectangular metre grid; short bounded Bezier joins; resampling at approximately 4m; uniform length normalization to 5,281m; flat game surface at y=1.2m; 15m nominal road width and game-specific runoff/collision meshes. Start point and traversal order are retained. Elevation, banking, road widths and facilities are simplified rather than a surveyed or laser-scanned reproduction.

All facility models, hotel gridshell, boats, floodlights and surface meshes are generated specifically in this Unity project. No commercial game's models, textures, logos or audio are imported. This is an unofficial game reconstruction, not an official licensed venue asset.
